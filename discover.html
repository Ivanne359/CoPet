ABOUT

Get to Know CoPet

For centuries, domestic ceramics held the warmth of hearth and family. Yet in modern pet care, feeding was reduced to clinical petroleum polymers and thin metallic clatter that slides across cold hardwood floors.

Vision

To become a trusted and recognized eco-friendly pet care brand that inspires sustainable lifestyles through innovative and environmentally friendly products.

Mission

To provide practical and affordable pet bowls made from coconut peat while minimizing agricultural waste, supporting local coconut suppliers, and promoting sustainable practices.

Goals

Short-Term

  • Introduce CoPet coconut peat pet bowl to the target market
  • Create brand awareness among pet owners and eco-conscious customers of CoPet
  • Form business alliances with local coconut suppliers

Long-Term

  • Expand product distribution to pet stores and online platforms
  • Produce additional eco-friendly pet products using coconut-based materials
  • Increase production facilities to meet high demand
  • Establish an excellent reputation as a sustainable pet care company

Why Choose CoPet?

CoPet provides an affordable and eco-friendly alternative to conventional pet bowls by
transforming discarded coconut husks into a useful everyday product. With its natural
brown appearance and simple, functional design, it offers a practical feeding solution for
pets while promoting sustainability, reducing coconut waste, and encouraging more
responsible use of natural resources.
